# When Does the Undercut Work? Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Detect every green-flag undercut attempt in 2023–26 dry races from cached FastF1 timing, then answer RQ1 (success rate and drivers), RQ2 (model vs rule of thumb, out of sample) and RQ3 (2026 vs 2023–25) in a WSAJ-format paper.

**Architecture:** A small Python package `undercut/` turns cached FastF1 sessions into one tidy lap table per race (`load.py`), finds attempts from lap-end crossing times (`attempts.py`), adds race-level features measured only on other cars (`features.py`), and fits/evaluates models (`models.py`). Thin scripts in `analysis/` write parquet to `data/` and CSVs to `results/`; `analysis/figures.py` and `paper/main.tex` consume `results/`.

**Tech Stack:** Python ≥ 3.12, uv, FastF1 (offline cache only), pandas, pyarrow, numpy, statsmodels, matplotlib, pytest, LaTeX (tectonic).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-17-undercut-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Execution starts after the Battery Battles submission (2026-11-16); nothing here may slow Battery Battles.
- FastF1 runs in `fastf1.Cache.offline_mode(True)` always; the archive returns 401 since ~2026-10-15 and no task may touch the network for timing data.
- Cache path: `~/Projects/battery-battles/data/cache`, overridable with env `UNDERCUT_FASTF1_CACHE`. Read it in place; never copy it and never write to or commit in `~/Projects/battery-battles`.
- Races: 2023 R1–22, 2024 R1–24, 2025 R1–24, 2026 R1–17 (Grands Prix only, no sprints).
- Attempt: gap at the line on lap n−1 in (0, **3.0 s**]; sensitivity 2 s and 5 s. Leader responds within **5 laps**. Success = chaser ahead at the end of the lap after the leader's out-lap; sensitivity one lap later.
- Dry races and green laps only (relaxed-green variant as a sensitivity check). Exclude intermediate/wet tyres and any car missing a lap before the check lap.
- Leakage rule: race-level features use other cars only; mechanism columns (`mech_*`) never enter a model.
- Methods stay at regression level; RQ3 is labelled exploratory everywhere it appears.
- Paper: LaTeX, double-spaced, line numbers, page numbers, no footnotes, APA citations, abstract < 250 words, IMRaD + Limitations + Reproducibility; the author writes the Discussion interpretations.
- `data/`, `results/*.parquet` and LaTeX build files are git-ignored; `results/*.csv`, `figures/*.pdf`, `paper/main.tex`, `paper/refs.bib` are committed.

## Review Focus

1. **Drive-through or stop-go penalties** have a `PitInTime` but no tyre change; a reasonable person expects them not to count as either car's stop. (Task 2 test `test_penalty_drive_through_is_not_a_stop`.)
2. **A delay category absent from a training fold** (e.g. no "4-5" response in 2023) makes the logit rank-deficient; LOSO should record NaN for that model in that fold and keep going, not crash. (Task 4 test `test_season_out_survives_rank_deficient_fold`.)
3. **A missing lap row in the middle of the window** (timing glitch, retirement) should drop the attempt, not raise or silently use the next lap. (Task 2 test `test_missing_lap_row_in_window_excludes_attempt`.)
4. **The leader pitting on the same lap as the chaser, or the lap before**, is not an undercut and not a no-response case. (Task 2 test `test_leader_pitting_same_lap_is_not_an_attempt`.)
5. **A race with no usable other-car stops** (e.g. one-stop race where only the pair stopped in a green window) gives NaN gain; the attempt must carry NaN `rule_pred` and be dropped from model comparison with the drop counted, not predicted as 0. (Task 3 test `test_no_other_stops_gives_nan_gain_and_rule`.)

### Task 2: Attempt detection

**Files:**
- Create: `undercut/attempts.py`, `tests/helpers.py`
- Test: `tests/test_attempts.py`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: the lap table from Task 1 (`DriverNumber, Driver, LapNumber, end_s, lap_time_s, pit_in, green, Compound, TyreLife`); `info` dict.
- Produces:
  - `WINDOW_S = 3.0`, `MAX_DELAY = 5`, `CHECK_OFFSET = 1`, `COLUMNS: list[str]`
  - `stops(laps) -> pd.DataFrame[DriverNumber, lap]`: the laps with `pit_in` where the next lap has `TyreLife` lower than this lap, or a different `Compound`. These are real tyre changes, so penalties are excluded.
  - `find_attempts(laps, info, window_s=WINDOW_S, max_delay=MAX_DELAY, check_offset=CHECK_OFFSET, require_green=True) -> pd.DataFrame` with exactly `COLUMNS`:
    - `race_id, year, rnd, chaser, leader` (the last two as DriverNumber ints)
    - `chaser_code, leader_code, lap` (n, the chaser's in-lap)
    - `gap_s, responded:bool, leader_pit_lap:float, delay:float, check_lap:float`
    - `success:float` (1/0, or NaN for no-response)
    - `chaser_ahead_end:float` (no-response only: is the chaser ahead at the end of lap n + max_delay + 1)
    - `chaser_age, leader_age` (TyreLife on lap n−1)
    - `chaser_old, chaser_new, leader_old` (compounds)
  - `tests/helpers.py: make_laps(cars: dict, green_off=(), drop=()) -> pd.DataFrame`. Each car is `dict(offset: float, laps: int, base: float, pits: {lap: new_compound}, compound: str, slow: {lap: seconds_added}, fresh: float)`. The car laps at `base` until its first stop and at `fresh` after it. `slow` adds in-lap and out-lap time. TyreLife resets to 1 on the out-lap. `end_s = offset + cumsum(lap times)`. `green_off` is a set of `(driver, lap)` pairs that aren't green, and `drop` removes rows.

The algorithm, which isn't fully pinned down by the signature:
- For each chaser stop at lap n, take the cars that have a lap n−1 row and sort them by `end_s` on lap n−1. The leader is the car immediately before the chaser.
- Both cars' laps n−1 and n must not be a stop or a pit-out for either car.
- m is the leader's first stop in n+1..n+max_delay. Then `delay = m − n` and `check_lap = m + 1 + check_offset`.
- Every lap n−1..check_lap must exist for both cars and use no wet compound.
- Green: with `require_green`, every lap n−1..check_lap is green for both cars. Without it, only n−1..m+1 needs to be.
- `success = end_s[chaser, check_lap] < end_s[leader, check_lap]`.
- With no leader stop in the window, the case is `responded=False`. It needs laps and green through n+max_delay+1.
- A wet race returns an empty frame with `COLUMNS`.

### Task 3: Features from other cars and mechanism columns

**Files:**
- Create: `undercut/features.py`
- Test: `tests/test_features.py`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `attempts.stops`, `attempts.COLUMNS` rows, the lap tables, and `tests/helpers.make_laps`.
- Produces:
  - `fresh_tyre_gain(laps, around_lap: int, exclude: set[int], window: int = 10) -> float`. For each other car's stop at lap p with |p − around_lap| ≤ window, laps p−2..p+3 all green, and all lap times present, compute mean(lap_time p−2, p−1) − mean(lap_time p+2, p+3). Return the median, or NaN if there are none. Positive means fresh tyres are faster.
  - `pit_loss(laps, exclude: set[int]) -> float`. For each other car's green stop at lap p, compute lap_time(p) + lap_time(p+1) − 2 × the median of that car's green, non-stop lap times in p−3..p+4 (excluding p and p+1). Return the median.
  - `deg_rate(laps, exclude: set[int]) -> float`. For each other car's stint with at least 6 green laps, excluding lap 1, in-laps and out-laps, fit the OLS slope of `lap_time_s` on `TyreLife`. Return the median, in seconds per lap. This is net of fuel burn, and the paper says so.
  - `add_features(attempts: pd.DataFrame, laps_by_race: dict[str, pd.DataFrame]) -> pd.DataFrame`. It adds:
    - `gain_s, pit_loss_s, deg_s_per_lap`, each with `exclude = {chaser, leader}` and `around_lap = lap`;
    - `margin_s = gain_s * delay − gap_s`;
    - `rule_pred`: 1.0 if `margin_s > 0`, 0.0 otherwise, NaN if `margin_s` is NaN;
    - `tyre_age_diff = leader_age − chaser_age`;
    - `compound_change` ∈ {"harder", "same", "softer"}, using the order SOFT < MEDIUM < HARD on `chaser_old → chaser_new`;
    - `delay_cat` ∈ {"1", "2", "3", "4-5"};
    - `y2026 = (year == 2026)` as an int;
    - `mech_inlap_loss = chaser lap_time(n) − leader lap_time(n)`;
    - `mech_outlap_gain = leader lap_time(n+1) − chaser lap_time(n+1)`;
    - `mech_traffic`: 1 if a car other than the leader crosses the end of lap n+1 within 1.5 s ahead of the chaser.

### Task 4: Models, metrics and out-of-sample splits; pre-registration tag

**Files:**
- Create: `undercut/models.py`, `notes/decisions.md`
- Test: `tests/test_models.py`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: the `add_features` output columns.
- Produces:
  - `DELAY_LEVELS = ["1", "2", "3", "4-5"]`, `CHANGE_LEVELS = ["harder", "same", "softer"]`
  - `FORMULA = "success ~ gap_s + delay_cat + tyre_age_diff + compound_change + pit_loss_s + deg_s_per_lap"`
  - `MODEL_COLS`: every column used by FORMULA, plus `margin_s` and `rule_pred`
  - `prepare(df) -> pd.DataFrame`: keeps `responded` rows, drops rows with NaN in `MODEL_COLS`, and sets `delay_cat` and `compound_change` to `pd.Categorical` with the fixed levels. The dropped count goes in `df.attrs["dropped"]`.
  - `fit_logit(df, formula=FORMULA, cluster=True)`: a binomial GLM. It raises `ValueError` on a rank-deficient design, as in `battles/stats.py`. With `cluster`, it uses `cov_type="cluster"` with groups = `race_id` codes.
  - `odds_table(res) -> pd.DataFrame[term, odds_ratio, ci_lo, ci_hi, p]`
  - `MODELS: dict[str, Callable[[train_df], Callable[[test_df], np.ndarray]]]`:
    - `base_rate`: predicts the training mean;
    - `rule`: predicts `rule_pred`, ignoring the training data;
    - `rule_cal`: a logit of `success ~ margin_s`;
    - `logit`: FORMULA, with no clustering, since only predictions are needed.
  - `metrics(y, p) -> dict[brier, logloss, accuracy]`, where accuracy uses p ≥ 0.5. Log loss clips p to [1e-6, 1 − 1e-6]. It's computed for `rule` too, but reported as not meaningful for a 0/1 rule.
  - `season_out(df, models, train_years, test_years) -> pd.DataFrame[race_id, year, y, <model>…]`: if a model's fit raises `ValueError`, its column is NaN for that split.
  - `loso(df, models, seasons=(2023, 2024, 2025)) -> pd.DataFrame`: `season_out` for each held-out season, then concatenated.
  - `bootstrap_by_race(df, fit, n=2000, seed=0, race_col="race_id") -> np.ndarray`, copied from `battles/stats.py`.
  - `bootstrap_metric_diff(pred, a, b, metric, n=2000, seed=0) -> tuple[float, float, float]`: the point estimate of metric(a) − metric(b), with a 95% CI from resampling races.

def test_bootstrap_diff_of_identical_models_is_zero():
